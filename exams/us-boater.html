Practice

When you must act to avoid a collision, how should the action be taken?
  1. Early, positively, and large enough for the other boater to notice
  2. At the last moment with a small course change
  3. Slowly and gradually, so the other boater is not startled by a sudden turn
  4. Only after sounding five short blasts

Explanation: Rule 8 says avoiding action must be positive, made in ample time and obvious to the other vessel. Small, late or hesitant changes make things worse.

Which color is the sidelight on the starboard (right) side of a boat?
  1. Green
  2. Red
  3. White
  4. Blue

Explanation: Starboard = green, port = red. Together they cover 112.5 degrees each, from dead ahead to just aft of abeam.

What is the stern?
  1. The back of the boat
  2. The front of the boat
  3. The right side
  4. The keel

Explanation: The stern (aft) is the back of the boat.
