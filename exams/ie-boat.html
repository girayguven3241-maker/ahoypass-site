Practise

When you change course to avoid a collision, how should you do it?
  1. Early and boldly, so the other boater can see it
  2. At the last moment with a slight turn
  3. By drifting slowly away from the track
  4. Only after the other boat sounds its horn

Explanation: Avoiding action must be positive and made in plenty of time. Small, late or hesitant changes can be missed by the other boater and may make things worse.

Which rules apply to a small leisure boat in open water?
  1. The same collision regulations as for a large ship
  2. Special relaxed rules for boats under ten metres
  3. Local custom set by the nearest yacht club
  4. Rules that apply only for boats with a licence

Explanation: The Collision Regulations (COLREGs) apply to every vessel on the sea. A small boat must not impede a vessel that can only navigate in a channel.

Which words introduce a distress call?
  1. Mayday, Mayday, Mayday
  2. Pan-Pan, Pan-Pan, Pan-Pan
  3. Securite, Securite, Securite
  4. Help, Help, Help

Explanation: Mayday is used when a person or vessel is in grave and imminent danger and needs immediate help.
