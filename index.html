Please note

Ahoypass is an independent practice app. It does not issue licences or certificates and is not affiliated with any government authority or exam operator. Questions and explanations are our own, except in Germany, where the officially published question catalogue (ELWIS) is used. Exam rules change, so always check the current rules of your authority.
